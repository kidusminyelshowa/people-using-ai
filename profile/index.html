03

You have access to powerful AI tools. Communication is how you make them useful.

Language, culture, context and the way you work all shape what AI tools can do for you. You are the bridge between technology and your reality. We exist to make that process simpler, helping you understand AI, communicate with it effectively and turning your access into meaningful use.

04

AI access has gone global. How people use it hasn’t caught up.

The way people understand, trust and use AI tools is shaped by where they live, the language they speak, the work they do and the context they operate in.

06

Between access and active use is a learning curve.

You don’t need to be a developer or technical expert to use AI well. If you are a professional, entrepreneur, freelancer or consultant with access to AI tools, you can learn to make them useful in the way you work.

We help you navigate that learning curve. Understanding the tools, communicating with them, experimenting and finding what works for you.

About us

We are communicators.

We understand people, make ideas clear and solve business problems. We’ve spent 20+ years doing this across marketing, strategy, operations and commercial growth in emerging markets.

09

We’ve also worked closely with technologists. As AI tools have evolved, we found ourselves overwhelmed by the way they were explained, often with the assumption that access to the tools meant knowing how to use them.

People using ai was born from wanting a space to learn and experiment, and to build the skills needed to keep up as the technology evolves.

10
Communication Critical thinking Creativity Judgement Problem-solving

Today, we bring that perspective to AI upskilling, training and research. We focus on the human skills that make technology useful: communication, critical thinking, creativity, judgement and problem-solving.

11

We’re building this from Nairobi, with lived experience of East Africa and its rapidly growing AI ecosystem. We want to help shape what AI becomes for people in emerging markets.

Get involved

There is limited research into how AI is understood, trusted and used across Africa and emerging markets.

In partnership with the University of Freiburg, we’re developing research through an anthropological lens to understand the people behind AI adoption in East Africa. We are exploring wide ranging topics from how people understand the AI technology, what shapes trust and behaviour, and how language, culture and context influence use.
