N.002The gapN.002

AI access has gone global. How people use it hasn’t caught up.

The way people understand, trust and use AI tools is shaped by four things:

01Where they live
02The language they speak
03The work they do
04The context they operate in

Language, culture, context and the way people work all shape what AI tools can do for them. People are the bridge between the technology and their reality.

N.007ResearchN.007
With the University of Freiburg

There is limited research into how AI is understood, trusted and used across Africa and emerging markets.

In partnership with the University of Freiburg, we are developing research through an anthropological lens to understand the people behind AI adoption in East Africa.

What we’re exploring

  1. 01How people understand AI technology
  2. 02What shapes trust and behaviour
  3. 03How language, culture and context influence use

N.008Our storyN.008

Why People using ai exists.

We’ve worked closely with technologists for years. As AI tools evolved, we found ourselves overwhelmed by the way they were explained, often with the assumption that access to the tools meant knowing how to use them.

People using ai was born from wanting a space to learn and experiment, and to build the skills needed to keep up as the technology evolves.

Today, we bring that perspective to AI upskilling, training and research.
